# ScamLedger Revenue Model v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the intended revenue model of ScamLedger.

The objective is to create a sustainable business model for the platform while maintaining transparent and clearly defined incentives for creators and participants.

The exact percentages and economic parameters are not finalized in this version.

---

## 2. Core Principle

ScamLedger is intended to earn revenue from providing launch infrastructure and related services.

The platform should not depend on participant losses as its primary source of revenue.

All applicable fees should be disclosed before participation and, where technically possible, enforced and recorded on-chain.

---

## 3. Revenue Sources

The initial revenue model may include:

### 3.1 Protocol Fee

ScamLedger may receive a predefined protocol fee from applicable platform activity.

The fee percentage and calculation method will be determined before implementation.

### 3.2 Launch Services

ScamLedger may charge creators for optional or required launch infrastructure and related services.

Possible services may include:

- Token deployment
- Launch configuration
- Contract verification
- Analytics
- Creator verification
- Multi-chain deployment

### 3.3 Premium Services

Additional optional services may be introduced as the platform develops.

Any additional fees should be clearly disclosed before the service is used.

---

## 4. Creator Revenue

Creators may receive a predefined and transparent share of applicable fees.

Creator revenue should be:

- Clearly disclosed before launch
- Defined by the launch configuration
- Subject to the platform's applicable rules
- Observable through on-chain transactions where technically possible

Creator revenue should not be arbitrarily changed after launch without the mechanism and conditions being publicly disclosed.

---

## 5. Liquidity

Liquidity is treated separately from ScamLedger revenue.

A future version of this document will define:

- Liquidity allocation
- Liquidity source
- Liquidity-provider ownership
- Locking or vesting mechanism
- Withdrawal conditions
- Liquidity-management rules

No liquidity percentage is finalized in v1.0.

---

## 6. Fee Transparency

Before a launch becomes active, the applicable economic parameters should be publicly disclosed.

This may include:

- Creator share
- ScamLedger protocol fee
- Liquidity allocation
- Other applicable allocations

The goal is for participants to understand the fee structure before interacting with the launch.

---

## 7. Future Economic Parameters

The following parameters remain under development:

- Protocol fee percentage
- Creator share percentage
- Liquidity allocation
- Launch fee, if applicable
- Premium service pricing
- Fee distribution mechanics

These parameters will be documented in a future version once finalized and tested.

---
