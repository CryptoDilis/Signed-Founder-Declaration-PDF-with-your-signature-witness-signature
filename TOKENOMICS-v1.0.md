# ScamLedger Tokenomics v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the initial tokenomics framework for ScamLedger.

The purpose is to provide a transparent structure for token supply, allocation, distribution, and ecosystem incentives.

The exact percentages and economic parameters are not finalized in this version.

---

## 2. Token Supply

The total token supply will be defined before the token generation event.

The final supply and token denomination will be publicly disclosed before launch.

---

## 3. Allocation Categories

The token allocation framework may include the following categories:

- Community
- Liquidity
- Ecosystem
- Treasury
- Development
- Team / Founders
- Partnerships
- Marketing
- Future incentives

Final allocation percentages will be documented before launch.

---

## 4. Community Allocation

A portion of the token supply may be allocated to the community.

Potential mechanisms may include:

- Community incentives
- Airdrops
- Ecosystem participation
- Rewards
- Future community programs

Any distribution rules will be publicly disclosed before implementation.

---

## 5. Liquidity Allocation

A portion of the token supply may be allocated for liquidity.

The final liquidity structure will specify:

- Liquidity allocation
- Liquidity source
- Liquidity-provider ownership
- Locking or vesting mechanism
- Withdrawal conditions
- Liquidity-management rules

Liquidity allocation is separate from ScamLedger platform revenue.

---

## 6. Team and Founder Allocation

Any allocation to founders, contributors, or team members will be publicly disclosed.

If applicable, team or founder allocations may be subject to:

- Vesting
- Lock-up periods
- Defined release schedules

The final terms will be disclosed before launch.

---

## 7. Treasury

A treasury allocation may be established to support the long-term development of the ScamLedger ecosystem.

Potential uses may include:

- Development
- Security
- Infrastructure
- Partnerships
- Ecosystem development
- Future initiatives

Treasury management rules will be documented separately.

---

## 8. Token Distribution

The final distribution mechanism will be publicly documented before launch.

This may include:

- Initial distribution
- Community distribution
- Liquidity provision
- Vesting schedules
- Ecosystem incentives

---

## 9. Transparency

Before launch, the following information should be publicly available:

- Total supply
- Allocation percentages
- Wallet addresses where applicable
- Vesting schedules
- Distribution rules
- Liquidity allocation
- Relevant token contracts

---

## 10. Future Parameters

The following parameters remain under development:

- Total supply
- Allocation percentages
- Initial circulating supply
- Vesting schedules
- Community allocation
- Liquidity allocation
- Treasury allocation
- Team allocation
- Distribution mechanics

These parameters will be finalized and documented in a future version.

---
